# Market research (summary)

## Discord casino bots

Community bots typically offer play-money minigames (coinflip, crash, mines) with leaderboards. Differentiation for ByteBits:

- **Provably fair** with published paytables (many bots use opaque RNG).
- **Premium UX** — consistent embeds, Activity (later), VIP cosmetics without real-money stakes.
- **Compliance-first** — no deposit/cashout in v1.

## Virtual economy benchmarks

- Daily faucets and starting balances reduce churn for new members.
- 4–5% house edge aligns with social casino RTP while keeping payouts understandable.
- Leaderboards by wagered and PNL drive engagement without pay-to-win.

## Crypto display (reference only)

Showing BTC/ETH spot prices (e.g. CoinGecko) can theme the lobby; **must not** imply credits are pegged or redeemable. Phase P4.

## Risks

- Discord policy on gambling imagery and monetization.
- IP for branded slot clones — use original ByteBits art only.
- Real-money expansion requires licensing, KYC, and payment partners — out of scope.
